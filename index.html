A few things to know

Watching, languages, the Starter Kit and this site.

Is it free to watch?

Yes. Every episode is free on YouTube, Instagram and Threads, and episodes also reach Facebook and TikTok over time. Following our channels is free too.

Which series are in English?

BEFORE YOU KNEW ME and DREAM ATLAS are in English. 陸橋 and 三點的實驗室 are in Chinese.

What is in the Starter Kit, and what language is it in?

The Suspense Shorts Starter Kit (懸疑短影音製作入門套件, version 1.1.0) is a ZIP with a 3-lesson tutorial, a 15-step production guide, story, shot-list and subtitle templates, a one-click editing tool and a finished 45-second example film with the prompts we used. The tutorial and templates are in Traditional Chinese; the generation prompts are in English. It costs NT$390 on Gumroad; early-bird code EARLY290 gives the first 30 buyers NT$290. Generation credits, voices, music and fonts are not included.

Can I get a refund on the Starter Kit?

Yes. Within 14 days of purchase you can get a full refund once, for any reason. Reply to your Gumroad receipt email or request it through Gumroad.

What is included in the free starter sample?

The free ZIP contains a complete fictional story, a storyboard with the first three shots filled in and six more rows to complete, a prompt example and blank worksheet, a quick-start guide and a usage licence. All downloadable sample content is in Traditional Chinese. No finished video or audio is included, and no email, payment or subscription is required.

Can I use the kit or the free sample for commercial videos?

Yes. Videos you make with either one may be published and monetized, including client work. The template, example, tutorial and tool files themselves may not be shared or resold, and the kit’s example film is for learning only. Each download includes its full licence. Check the terms of any third-party tools or assets you use as well.

Why are some episodes missing a Facebook link?

Facebook limits how often a page can post, so episodes reach our Facebook page later than YouTube, Instagram and Threads. New links appear here automatically as each episode is posted.

Are the stories based on real events?

No. All four series are original fiction made with AI-assisted visuals; their characters and events are invented. 陸橋 is inspired by a real 2025 discovery, the identification of a Denisovan jawbone dredged from the sea floor off Penghu, but its characters are fictional and its timeline is compressed for drama.

Does this site take payments or collect personal information?

No. This site has no forms, sign-ups or analytics trackers. The Starter Kit is sold through Gumroad, which handles checkout, payment, tax and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. Social and video links open third-party platforms, where their own terms apply.
